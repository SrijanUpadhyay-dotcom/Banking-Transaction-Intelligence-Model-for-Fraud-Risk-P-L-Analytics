P&L Formula Reference
Net Revenue
= Fee Income + Interchange − Processing Cost
Net P&L Impact
= Net Revenue − CB Loss − Refund Loss − Fraud Loss
Fraud Loss Rate (%)
= (Fraud Loss / Gross TX Value) × 100
Risk-Adjusted Revenue
= Net Revenue − Fraud Loss − Chargeback Loss
Revenue Leakage %
= |MIN(0, Net P&L)| / Fee Income × 100
Cost-to-Income Ratio
= Processing Cost / (Fee + Interchange) × 100
MoM Variance %
= (Current − Prior P&L) / |Prior P&L| × 100
Exception Rate (%)
= High-Risk Txns / Total Txns × 100

Classification: RESTRICTED | For Senior Management
TO: Senior VP, Fraud Risk & Financial Intelligence
FROM: Transaction Monitoring & Analytics Team

RE: BANKING TRANSACTION INTELLIGENCE — FRAUD, RISK & P&L ANALYTICS REVIEW
1. Executive Summary
A comprehensive analytics review of 50,000 banking transactions across digital, card, ATM, branch, and payment channels was conducted for the fiscal period January 2023 – December 2024. The analysis identified statistically significant fraud clusters, revenue leakage patterns, and P&L variances requiring immediate executive attention and targeted risk controls. Approximately 5.0% of transactions were confirmed as fraudulent, with concentrated losses in Card Not Present, Account Takeover, and Authorised Push Payment typologies.
2. Key Findings
▸ Fraud Loss Rate of 1.21% of Gross Transaction Value — above industry benchmark of 0.8%.
▸ Crypto Exchange and Gaming/Gambling merchant categories show chargeback ratios 3.2× above portfolio average.
▸ Off-hours transactions (00:00–05:59) exhibit a fraud rate 4.7× higher than daytime transactions.
▸ USSD and API/Open Banking channels exhibit the highest fraud concentration per transaction volume.
▸ Revenue leakage from refunds and reversals represents approximately 18.4% of total fee income.
▸ Machine Learning (Isolation Forest + Random Forest ensemble) achieved ROC-AUC of 0.96 on held-out test data.
3. P&L Impact Summary
▸ Gross Transaction Value: $4.82B | Net Revenue: $12.4M | Net P&L Impact: -$84.5M
▸ Fraud Loss: $58.2M | Chargeback Loss: $38.7M | Refund Loss: $3.2M
▸ P&L compression estimated at 22–28% vs. gross fee income due to fraud, chargebacks, and refund losses.
▸ Corporate and Private Banking segments represent 63% of total fraud loss despite 13% of transaction count.
4. Recommended Actions
1. Implement enhanced transaction velocity controls for USSD and API channels.
2. Deploy real-time device fingerprinting and IP geolocation for all Card Not Present transactions above $500.
3. Escalate 500-transaction exception queue to Level 2 investigation — estimated exposure exceeds materiality threshold.
4. Engage Crypto Exchange and Gaming merchants for enhanced monitoring or volume restriction.
5. Commission full reconciliation review on the 12% of transactions with balance inconsistency flags.
5. Next Steps
▸ Q1: Promote ML ensemble to production transaction monitoring stack (target <2% FPR, >85% recall).
▸ Q1: Integrate BTI Terminal into senior management reporting cadence (weekly P&L + fraud pack).
▸ Q2: Commission external benchmark comparison against LexisNexis / FraudNet industry data.
▸ Q2: Review and update 19-rule fraud engine based on Q1 model performance metrics.
